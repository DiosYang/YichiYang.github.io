## Overview

I worked on quantitative research into industry rotation, combining market data, time-series indicators, and trading simulations. The focus was to translate statistical behavior into candidate trading rules and examine how those rules performed across industries.

## Industry Rotation Research

Working with the team, I compared movements in the Hurst exponent with standard Bollinger Bands. The Hurst exponent provided a way to investigate persistence and long-range dependence in return series, while Bollinger Bands supplied a complementary reference for price variation.

## Data Preparation and Statistical Analysis

I collected historical data from Wind, including the CSI 300, the SSE Composite Index, and industry indices. I organized index histories in SQL and used Python to investigate indicator behavior over time.

Using least-squares linear regression, I examined the typical range and fluctuations of the Hurst exponent and explored thresholds for candidate buying and selling rules.

## Strategy Simulation

I simulated stock transactions across different industries based on changes in the Hurst exponent, calculated expected returns, and compared the behavior of the proposed rules. This work provided a practical view of how a statistical signal could be tested as part of an industry rotation strategy.
