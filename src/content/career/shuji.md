## Overview

At Shuji, I worked on forecasting problems in electricity consumption, industrial energy demand, and commodity markets. The work connected data preparation and time-series modeling with planning needs across different forecasting horizons.

## Electricity and Energy Consumption Forecasting

I extracted and prepared historical electricity load, industrial energy consumption, and weather data. Feature engineering included lagged observations, rolling averages, seasonal indicators, and external variables such as holidays and policy changes.

I compared LSTM, CNN-LSTM, XGBoost, LightGBM, and Random Forest models, using grid search and Optuna to tune hyperparameters. This work involved bringing heterogeneous inputs into a consistent modeling dataset and evaluating alternative forecasting approaches.

## Commodity Price Forecasting

I worked with a VMD–SSA–LSTM framework for forecasting commodity prices related to the coal chemical industry. Variational Mode Decomposition and Singular Spectrum Analysis were used to extract trend and cyclical components before LSTM forecasting. The approach explored how decomposing a complex time series could support prediction.

## Forecasting Across Time Horizons

I applied temporal hierarchy methods to electricity consumption in Ordos, developing forecasts at daily, monthly, and annual resolutions. The objective was to maintain consistency between short-term projections and longer-term energy planning, and to translate longer-term consumption targets into shorter-term planning inputs.
