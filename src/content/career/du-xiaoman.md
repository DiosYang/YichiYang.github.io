## My Perspective on Credit Risk

Credit risk management balances portfolio risk with lending volume. My focus is to identify concentrated pockets of high risk, so that targeted intervention can protect the portfolio while preserving lending capacity for other customers.

I develop both model-based variables and interpretable behavioral tags. Models can capture complex patterns, but depend on historical repayment outcomes and may become less reliable as the external environment changes. Behavioral tags can support earlier responses to emerging risks. I assess both through their risk differentiation, timeliness, stability, coverage, and the business cost of acting on them.

## Selected Work

### Multiple-Lender Borrowing

I worked on a component that derives risk variables from external inquiry data to assess borrowing across multiple lenders. From October 2025, changes in the lending-assistance market coincided with institutions exiting and a marked shift in the underlying data. The model-derived variables showed strong risk differentiation, but this shift also highlighted the need to reassess their stability and interpretation as the external environment changed.

### Borrowing to Repay Existing Loans

I worked on identifying customers whose repayment depended heavily on access to new borrowing. In a June 2026 case, a lending-assistance platform failure was followed by funding-partner losses and tighter credit availability. We assessed that customers with pronounced reliance on new loans to service existing debt could face heightened risk. Identifying this segment supported targeted policy rejection ahead of potential deterioration in observed repayment performance.
