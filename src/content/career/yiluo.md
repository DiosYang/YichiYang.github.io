## Overview

My internship combined fundamental company research with data preparation and scenario analysis. I studied how industry conditions, company operations, and policy changes could affect future earnings and valuation.

## Company and Industry Research

I prepared in-depth research reports on Sany Heavy Industry and AVIC Xi'an Aircraft Industry Group, examining five years of financial information, contract data, equipment replacement cycles, and upstream and downstream industry relationships.

The analysis drew on Wind, annual reports, and industry databases. Relevant inputs included capacity utilization, order backlogs, raw material prices, transportation indicators such as the Baltic freight index, and policy developments.

## Forecasting and Valuation

For the aircraft manufacturer, I constructed revenue projections using delivery volumes, unit prices, and changes in gross margins. I incorporated policy-related assumptions into sensitivity analysis to examine how different conditions could affect growth.

I used P/E, P/B, ROE decomposition, and discounted cash flow methods to assess valuation. Scenario-based earnings projections and valuation ranges helped make the underlying assumptions and investment risks explicit.

## Research Data Infrastructure

I collected and cleaned company and industry data to support the research process. Using Python and SQL, I also built a historical database covering approximately 200 stocks in the machinery sector, with automated updates for intraday trading data.
